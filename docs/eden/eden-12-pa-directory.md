# EDEN-12 · Pennsylvania directory acceptance

**The existing [protected Preview](https://ourground-staging-git-eden10-penuels-projects-aa802b06.vercel.app) is suitable for a limited pilot. It is not a statewide food directory.** On November 7, 2026, it contained **324 Philadelphia food listings and 43 healthcare sites in 15 municipalities**. EDEN-12 imported **zero** new listings. A fresh comparison of the two authorized publishers found all 367 source bindings unchanged, with no new eligible Philadelphia records, withdrawals or conflicts in the pilot. No database edits, redeployment, change to EDEN, Gemini call, billing action or production change was needed.

Mocked, allowlisted tool proposals drove the **real deployed controller, UI, API and database**. Desktop checked home, Find Support, Give Support, For Organizations and About, including context on returning to search. Philadelphia and Pittsburgh marker/list selection worked in both directions. Chrome received seven successful vector-tile responses on desktop and two in the mobile gap check; neither run called a Gemini endpoint or used Geoapify to search for the listings. [Desktop evidence](verification/eden-12/hosted-desktop.json) · [mobile gap checks](verification/eden-12/hosted-mobile-gaps.json).

**What EDEN actually said matters.** Search reported the loaded count and cautioned that coverage varies. Both empty food pages explicitly said help might still exist and offered Feeding America, 211 and USAGov links as **external referrals**, not local results. A separate “read results” proposal after an empty search still told the reviewer to search first. It did not invent a provider, but it did not convey the referral advice. Treat this as an acceptance gap for conversational use: that follow-up should eventually distinguish zero matches from no completed search and from a network failure. Per owner instruction, EDEN's implementation was not changed. Focused local mock tests verified that the existing agent's fact selection distinguishes a confirmed zero from a failed query, and rejects unapproved records. No hosted inference or voice evaluation is claimed.

## Owner outreach: ready to send, not sent

- Dauphin/Cumberland: [Central PA Agency Services](https://www.centralpafoodbank.org/agency-resources/), **arinfo@centralpafoodbank.org**, 717-564-1700. Ask for the directory steward rather than applying to distribute food.
- Allegheny and nearby counties: [Greater Pittsburgh Partner Network](https://pittsburghfoodbank.org/about/contact-us/), **412-460-6562**. Ask who can license the locator's public-site subset and authorize partner redisplay.
- Statewide fallback: [PA 211 partnership contact form](https://www.pa211.org/contact/), United Way of Pennsylvania. Ask which regional centers own the relevant data, whether an export is permitted, and who can issue the written NDP authorization. The provider listing-update form is not a data license.

Suggested request: “Our Ground is a student-built Pennsylvania resource directory. May we pilot a free, source-attributed subset of **public food distribution sites** in Dauphin or Allegheny and neighboring communities on a protected staging site? Please identify which public facility fields may be stored, mapped, backed up and displayed, and whether a later public portfolio site, screenshots or GitHub redistribution would be allowed separately. We seek stable site IDs, source dates, public pickup coordinates, organization contact channels, correction and withdrawal notices, required credit, refresh frequency, retention/termination terms, any fees, and a responsible steward. We do not request intake, client, volunteer, personal contact or confidential-location data.”

Archive an affirmative agreement privately. Do not treat silence, trial access or staging permission as consent to publish an export, code fixture, demo recording or public listing. Review a small dated export and proposed exclusions before adding a source.

## Maintenance and production decision

Use the [operator runbook](pa-directory-maintenance.md). The [live feed comparison](verification/eden-12/feed-comparison.json) found zero proposed changes and no outstanding flags. These timestamps establish when source data was observed, **not** that any site is open, has food or is accepting patients. The protected database occupies **68,310,707 bytes (68.31 MB; about 13.7% of the [500 MB Free allowance](https://supabase.com/pricing))**. Application indexes occupy 32,858,112 bytes. Fourteen restricted-role PostgreSQL measurements ranged from **0.43 to 14.53 ms**; these are warm individual queries, not network latency or a load test. The runtime could not read raw source bindings or correction reports. The limiter cleanup job remained active. Anonymous homepage and health requests both [redirected to Vercel sign-in](verification/eden-12/access.json). The existing 367-record external backup's [ciphertext digest matched](verification/eden-12/backup-check.json); no restore was repeated.

**Production remains a no-go.** Assign a steward and backup, exercise a real correction/withdrawal and separately recoverable restore before public use, confirm keys/retention/monitoring and map-quota protections, and describe food coverage accurately. Expanded regional coverage requires permission or an independently reusable current source with usable public points. The empty-result conversational follow-up and hosted Gemini audience/privacy gates remain open. No unattended refresh or publication is active.
