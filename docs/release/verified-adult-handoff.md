# Verified adult handoff

The handoff is off by default. The ordinary 19+ acknowledgment and its eight-hour denial remain in effect. If enabled, a denied visitor can ask an adult to choose **Verify as an adult**. The adult reviews the disclosure, completes Yoti’s hosted document scan, and checks the result after returning. Find Support is the default destination, and Give Support is preserved when it was the starting page. The handoff preserves the denial cookie and creates a separate grant for up to 15 minutes, bounded by the denial’s expiry. The adult should choose **Finish adult session** before returning the device. Verification establishes eligibility at the time of the check. It cannot tell who uses the device afterward.

Yoti’s Age Verification Service supports configurable thresholds and a document scan with authenticity, selfie matching and passive liveness checks. The integration requests the threshold of 19 and accepts only a matching, completed document result fetched directly from Yoti. Our Ground receives the threshold outcome and opaque references. It does not request or store the birth date, ID, selfie or biometric template. Yoti processes the document and selfie under its own notice. The grant permits public directory access and existing consented Eden text and Read Reply. Organization management, private reports and new Live calls are outside the grant. [Document scan](https://developers.yoti.com/age-verification/doc-scan) · [Result contract](https://developers.yoti.com/age-verification/headless-idv-results)

Before activation, the owner needs to:

1. Verify the organization in Yoti Hub and obtain Age Verification Service access for hosted U.S. Doc Scan. Confirm the supported IDs, document authenticity, selfie matching, passive liveness, and threshold-only results. Obtain written confirmation that a configurable 19+ check denies the day before the 19th birthday and accepts the 19th birthday and later, including the applicable date, time zone, and leap-day rule. The public documentation uses `OVER` and does not specify the birthday boundary. [AVS onboarding](https://developers.yoti.com/age-verification/onboarding) · [Document scan](https://developers.yoti.com/age-verification/doc-scan)
2. Obtain written pricing for submitted documents, unsuccessful checks, retries, quotas and any setup or support fees. Confirm the acceptable-use and data-processing terms, including whether the anonymous 15-minute shared-device grant across the supported routes counts as one continuous visit. [Yoti Client Terms](https://www.yoti.com/terms/age-verification/organisations/)
3. Obtain an authorized sandbox SDK ID and RSA private key with Central Authentication OAuth access through a secret channel. In a protected test setup, validate session creation, the hosted return, and authenticated results for success, failure, cancellation, and expiry using Yoti’s separate sandbox API. The sandbox simulates outcomes. Arrange provider-approved acceptance for actual document capture, liveness and the birthday boundary separately. [Sandbox guide](https://developers.yoti.com/age-verification/sandbox-integration-guide)
4. Approve the hosted biometric consent and the public disclosure, use and retention policy. Confirm provider retention and deletion and verify how hosting runtime logs and drains handle opaque callback references. Check keyboard, screen reader, visible focus, timeout, and the camera, ID and QR steps. Provide accessible support and confirm any required non-biometric alternative. [Yoti AVS privacy](https://www.yoti.com/privacy/age-verification/) · [U.S. biometrics policy](https://www.yoti.com/terms/biometrics-policy-us/) · [Accessibility statement](https://www.yoti.com/accessibility-statement/age-verification/)
5. Review a protected deployment candidate and record the public deployment as the rollback target. Provision server-only `YOTI_AVS_SDK_ID` and `YOTI_AVS_API_KEY` for the canonical production project, approve a global daily cap with `ADULT_HANDOFF_DAILY_LIMIT`, and confirm the existing Redis service. Leave `ADULT_HANDOFF_ENABLED` off pending explicit cutover approval. Recheck the original age gate before enabling or deploying.

For rollback, disable the feature in the release configuration and restore the previous verified deployment if needed. The signed original denial does not depend on the provider or handoff records.
