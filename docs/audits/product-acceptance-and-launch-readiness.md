## Accessibility, performance and visual polish

- **Keyboard and reflow.** The first Tab reached “Skip to content,” and Enter moved focus to the main landmark on desktop and mobile. At 320 CSS pixels, Home, Give Support, For Organizations, Account and Find Support had no document-level horizontal overflow. Search, pagination and source details remained usable when map tiles failed. Screen-reader announcement order, physical iOS/Android touch, increased text size and the signed-in forms still need hands-on acceptance.
- **Automated accessibility.** Axe-core 4.10.3 checked WCAG A/AA rules through 2.1 on the sampled pages. It flagged the Food button at 1.37:1 in one mobile interaction state. A later settled touch sample showed dark-on-light and light-on-dark category colors. Retest the focus, hover and transition states before sign-off. The legacy globe’s `#globeMount` also has an ARIA label on a generic element. Several contrast checks required manual review. These scans do not establish WCAG conformance.
- **Cold loads.** Chrome used an empty-cache desktop profile and a 390-pixel mobile profile with 80 ms latency and a 5 Mbps downlink. A desktop homepage filmstrip run recorded LCP at **5.21 s** and one mobile homepage visit at **2.64 s**. Give Support measured **0.99 s desktop / 0.38 s mobile** and Find Support **0.74 s / 1.58 s**. Several homepage visits supplied no paint entry. The mobile viewport requested the same 1672-pixel hero asset as desktop. Timings include protected hosting and headless-browser overhead.
- **Stability, interaction and flicker.** Initial Find Support CLS was 0.007 desktop and 0.014 mobile in the isolated samples. The desktop workflow reached **0.229** after results and map interactions. Where Event Timing supplied entries, sampled interactions in the isolated runs ranged from 32 to 88 ms. This is not a field INP measurement. The desktop filmstrip and image-state sampling showed delayed first appearance, with no repeated disappearance of the loaded hero. Frame capture has gaps. Intermittent flicker on a physical device remains unverified. Prioritize the missed tap and accessibility findings ahead of decorative motion.

There is no field p75 for LCP, INP or CLS in this audit. Google's good thresholds are 2.5 s, 200 ms and 0.1 respectively at the 75th percentile by device class. Treat the homepage paint delay and directory movement as investigation targets, not a field pass or fail. ## Directory stewardship and recovery

The staging runtime's published view returned **367 imported, unverified records**. Philadelphia has all **324 food sites**. The **43 HRSA healthcare sites** cover 15 municipalities, including Philadelphia, Pittsburgh, Harrisburg, Steelton and Mechanicsburg. The database measured **68,310,707 bytes**, about 13.7% of the documented 500 MB allowance. At 10 miles, EDEN-13 measured Philadelphia Food 323, Pittsburgh Food 0, Harrisburg Food 0 and Harrisburg Healthcare 3. At five miles Harrisburg Healthcare returned 2. These are counts around a selected point. They do not establish hours, appointment availability or the absence of other community services. [Published view and capacity](product-acceptance-evidence.json) · [Regional coverage](../eden/eden-12-pa-directory.md)

Philadelphia’s source timestamp is November 5 and HRSA’s is November 6. Both sets were imported November 7. EDEN-12 compared the two publisher feeds with the 367 bindings and found no pending differences. The Philadelphia catalog identifies the City license and its terms. HRSA attribution and suppressed-address handling are documented for the permitted subset. The two regional food banks and PA 211 remain referral or permission-request paths. There is no redistribution agreement for their listings. Preserve source links, timestamps, attribution and uncertainty. About and Find Support currently describe US-wide discovery more broadly than the pilot inventory justifies. [City dataset and license](https://opendataphilly.org/datasets/free-food-and-meal-sites/) · [Source decisions and permissions](../eden/eden-12-pa-directory.md)

The refresh tool compares source IDs and hashes, records additions, changes and missing entries, and flags conflicts, privacy holds and stale observations for human disposition. A missing row does not automatically close or delete a listing. The runbook calls for daily comparisons, weekly ordinary review, monthly rights checks and prompt withdrawal review. The owner is identified as interim privacy operator. A named directory steward and backup, monitored correction inbox/queue, and exercised correction-to-withdrawal process were not evidenced. No unattended publication is configured. [Maintenance runbook](../eden/pa-directory-maintenance.md)

The independent encrypted 367-row archive on Penuel Back matched its recorded ciphertext checksum during this audit. Its plaintext integrity had already been verified. The current archive has not been restored into a clean replacement target. The earlier successful restore covered three rows in the same staging cluster. The directory archive intentionally omits Auth users, correction reports, claims, revisions and memberships. A separately recoverable decryption key, recovery owner and a plan for corrections and holds accumulated after backup still need confirmation. An application rollback cannot recover them. ## Security and release options

The Vercel project is `ourground-staging` on Node 24. Anonymous requests to both the homepage and health endpoint redirected to Vercel Authentication. Authenticated health returned 200. All 20 project variables targeted Preview only, with zero hidden Production variables. The database URL, CA and limiter secret have sensitive storage. No owner URL or Gemini key was in the project settings checked. The runtime connected as `our_ground_api` and lacked raw table reads for services, reports and limiter buckets. API health returned `no-store`. Page and API headers restricted framing, cameras and microphones. The previous package scan found no matches for the tested private credentials. [Current checks](product-acceptance-evidence.json) · [EDEN-13 package evidence](../eden/verification/eden-13/preview.json)

The unchanged PostgreSQL limiter has earlier concurrency and fail-closed evidence. Six of 18 parallel database-backed admissions succeeded, and holding the admission lock produced HTTP 503. The current artifact previously returned and recovered from a real hosted 429. The staging Geoapify browser key restricts other browser origins, but a key holder can omit browser headers and consume shared quota. The owner accepted this for protected review only. A short grouped runtime-log query supplied no groups, so continuous alerting, long-term retention and provider log redaction are unverified. Search keywords can appear in the address bar and copied resource link. The audit confirmed this with “share.” The hosted Privacy page also has no working public contact link. These are concrete decisions for a public pilot that lets people search for sensitive needs or submit free-text corrections. [Limiter and map limitations](../eden/eden-10-postgres-staging-limiter.md) · [Keyword/share and headers evidence](product-acceptance-evidence.json)

**Directory and donor referrals.** A limited public pilot is feasible once the gates below pass. A Philadelphia-food and selected PA healthcare scope matches the inventory. A public platform deployment needs its own environment, credentials, quota policy, monitoring, and explicit release decision. Preserve the authenticated reviewer Preview and its rollback artifact.

**Organization accounts.** Keep onboarding outside that pilot unless the full flow is proven with controlled identities, from verified sign-in through claim review, scoped edit, publication, revocation and deletion. Align the organization entry page with whichever service is actually offered.

**Gemini-powered EDEN.** Hosted session, speech and Live token requests all returned `403 EDEN_DISABLED`. Google's additional terms restrict API clients directed toward or likely to be accessed by people under 18. Unpaid-service terms also raise product-improvement and human-review issues for sensitive resource-seeking conversations. A public directory can serve the intended audience independently. A future hosted AI proposal needs a compatible audience/provider agreement and tested consent, data handling, abuse and cost controls. The previously evaluated adult local environment provides no public entitlement.
